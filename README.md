# AQEF Textbook — AI Quality Engineering

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**A textbook on AI Quality Engineering: how to decide what to test in an AI system, how far
to trust the instruments that judge it, and how to turn their results into a release decision.**

First edition 1.0 · 2026-10 · by Igor Akymenko

> **Informative.** This book explains the AQEF specification; it is not part of it and adds
> no requirement. It is written against **AQEF 1.0-CR1** and **CTS 1.0-CR1**. Where the book
> and the specification differ, the specification is right.

## What this book covers

- **Part I. The Problem and the Model** — why AI systems need a different approach; quality
  dimensions and risk; AQEF's principles and architecture; Conformance and its limits.
- **Part II. What to Test and How** — Scenarios, Quality Contracts, data and Evidence,
  coverage and traceability.
- **Part III. Evaluation, and Trust in It** — Validators, Judges and Human Review; Judge
  Admission and Calibration; Seeded Controls; panels of Judges.
- **Part IV. Application Areas** — RAG, agents and multi-turn dialogue; safety, regression
  and operational quality.
- **Part V. From Results to a Decision** — aggregation, Quality Gates, reporting and the
  release decision.

Capabilities that are **At Risk** or **Reserved** in AQEF 1.0-CR1 are labelled where they
appear.

  Candidate Recommendation review is running.
